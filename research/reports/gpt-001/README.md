## F_LR 0.388 → 0.692 (point 6)
- F_LR = min(λ_left, λ_right) / μ (μ 0.58), the final wall's tilting-table limit. Both of GPT's
  sides are higher than Paper's. The weaker side is the right in both walls (0.401 vs 0.225).
- F_LR tracks the LLC score of each wall's last selected candidate, the same tilting-table limit on the
  pre-stabilisation partial wall, quantised in λ steps of 0.05. It matches to within one quantisation
  step: GPT 0.690 → 0.692, Paper 0.345 → 0.388, Fake ReAct 0.172 → 0.168.
- Along the walls (trajectory.csv), the partial-wall LLC is not monotonic:
  - GPT ≥ Paper from step 6 to 13;
  - equal at 14 and 16;
  - GPT lower at 17–18 (0.172 vs 0.517/0.345);
  - GPT higher only at the last stone (0.690 vs 0.345).

  So GPT's wall was not more stable than Paper's throughout; the final value is set by the final
  configuration.
- At step 20, GPT's last change, both candidates of that same state had LLC 0.690. By this coarse,
  pre-stabilisation proxy, that one switch did not decide the final limit.
- The two walls share no state after step 6. The difference therefore belongs to the whole trajectory
  from step 6 on (9 changes and every later sample), not to an identifiable decision.
- Density and height alone do not explain it: Fake ReAct has the densest, lowest wall (0.695, 161)
  and the lowest F_LR (0.168).
- **Attributing the F_LR gain to a decision needs counterfactual branches**: at each of the 9 change
  points, the Paper candidate instead of GPT's, then a continuation. A Paper continuation is free (no
  API) but builds new walls; a GPT continuation is paid. Neither was run.

## Conclusions
1. The live pipeline is sound on a full wall: complete GPT control, exact billing ($0.14, 37 requests),
   PASS on replay and geometry, and nothing leaked.
2. GPT departed from Paper in 9 of 16 decisions, always with a real change of placement, consistently
   favouring NHR/GDS-first orders (in-band, level placements, smaller stones mid-wall early on).
3. On this one wall, F_LR rose by 0.30 and F_AH worsened by 0.59. F_SF, F_AV and completion stayed
   about equal. n = 1: these are observations, not effects.
4. The F_LR difference is not attributable to any single decision from these records. It equals the
   final wall's limit, which GPT's partial wall reached only at its last stone.
5. Before more GPT walls are compared on quality, a free counterfactual study (Paper continuation
   at the 9 change points) would show which changes move F_LR and F_AH.
